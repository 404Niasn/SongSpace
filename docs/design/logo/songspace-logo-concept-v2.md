## 提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a fresh logo concept for 歌境 SongSpace, a music collecting and miniature-world cycling game. The user disliked the previous generic glossy blue music-player icon and requests lighter colors. This is a completely new design, NOT a recolor.
Core idea: a curled vinyl record becomes an inviting doorway to a tiny world. Draw one ingenious compact graphic: a softly circular record with a broad segment peeled upward like a curled page, forming a small archway. The curled record is the portal itself. Through its opening show just one simple rolling mint hill and a tiny pale apricot sun, with generous negative space. Two quiet curved groove lines establish the record. Prioritize a distinctive unified silhouette and playful optical balance. Avoid collage. The mark should feel like an inventive independent cozy-game brand, not a corporate audio player.
Style: high quality flat vector-style logo, charming clever geometry, broad rounded forms, clean sharp edges, restrained two or three colors, no outlines thinner than the main features. Colors: pale sage/mint green as primary, warm ivory and mist blue, one tiny soft apricot accent. Use muted medium teal where necessary for clear separation and legibility. Entire appearance light, airy and friendly. No dark navy, no black record, no saturated neon, no chrome, no glossy gradients, no 3D render, no shadows, no S-shaped ribbon road, no conventional music-note icon or headphones.
Composition: a single large centered logo mark on a clean warm off-white square background, with abundant negative space. NO app tile boundary, no border. NO text, no lettering, no watermark, no mockup or presentation board. Render a polished standalone logo concept that reads well at small size.
