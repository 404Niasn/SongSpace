# 歌境 Logo 初稿：循歌入境

使用内置 imagegen 生成。创意：唱片与 S 形旅途融为同一个图形，中孔化为日出，呼应歌曲收藏、骑行和音乐世界。当前为设计概念 PNG，尚未替换应用 Logo。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a genuinely distinctive app logo for 歌境 / SongSpace, a playful music collecting game where songs become miniature worlds and you cycle through music landscapes. Deliver one polished standalone app icon, NOT a presentation board.
Creative concept: "a record you can travel through". One bold continuous ribbon curves into a compact circular vinyl record, with an S-shaped negative-space winding path passing through its center. The central record hole doubles as a tiny rising sun at the end of the path. The record's lower contour subtly becomes a small landscape/horizon. Make the symbolism inseparable, clever and immediately legible, not a collage of separate icons. No literal bicycle or tiny decorative scenery.
Style: premium contemporary graphic identity, soft geometric construction, beautifully balanced optical spacing, restrained playful personality, iconic silhouette, strong readability at 32px. Flat vector-like shapes with just a restrained spectral highlight. Current app identity uses fresh mint, ice blue, pearl white. Use deep ink blue for strong contrast with mint and light ice-blue ribbon accents. Avoid generic music note in circle, headphones, Spotify-like waves, rainbow overload, 3D chrome, visual clutter.
Composition: square canvas with softly rounded pearl-white app-icon tile filling most of canvas, a large centered original mark with generous inner clear space. Outer canvas transparent alpha. No cast shadow, no mockup, no words or letters, no watermark. The mark should be ready to use as an application icon.
